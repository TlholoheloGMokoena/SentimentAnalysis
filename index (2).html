How the Sentiment Analysis Works

  1. You provide text (CSV, typed or pasted).
  2. The text is cleaned: lower-cased, URLs and extra punctuation removed.
  3. Words are split into tokens and matched to a dictionary of words with emotional weights.
  4. The model adds up the weights, flipping them after words like “not” and boosting after “very”.
  5. The total becomes a score from −1 to +1, which maps to Positive, Neutral or Negative.
  6. Results are counted and grouped by date, category and keyword.
  7. Charts and plain-English insights are written from those numbers.
